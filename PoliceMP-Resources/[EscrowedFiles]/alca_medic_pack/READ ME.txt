HOW TO INSTALL -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Put alca_medic_pack folder in your resource folder
- Add "start alca_medic_pack" in your server.cfg
- Add props name in your dpemote or script (DON'T FORGET TO CREATE OBJECT AND ATTACH IT TO THE PED. YOUR SCRIPT CAN'T 
GUESS ALONE WHAT YOU WANT FROM IT)

HELP FOR SCRIPT-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you prefer to make it in your own script, don't forget to create the object, call the animation, attach the prop to the ped and delete it at the end of animation (the script can't guess alone) 

use  AttachEntityToEntity, RequestAnimDict, TaskPlayAnim, StopAnimTask, DetachEntity, DeleteEntity, CreateObject
you can find everything on CFX forum or google
