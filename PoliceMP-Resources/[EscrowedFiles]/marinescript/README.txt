This script adds a boat GPS and diving computer to FiveM.
In the Boat GPS, it displays the current co-ordinates of the boat, the current knots that the boat is travelling at, the heading, the depth of water beneath the boat, the current time, and the anchor status.
The anchor stops the boat from moving around by about a metre to allow for slack.

For diving, the script adds it so that the player has a limited amount of air in their tank. It also displays a dive computer with air left percentage, time of dive, depth of player and heading.
The dive tank can be replaced when off of dive mode.
There is also a respiration noise when diving, and a better swimming animation as if the player is using flippers.

Commands:
/boatui - enables boat ui
/anchor - anchors boat
/dive - begins dive
/divetank - changes air tank when not diving.
