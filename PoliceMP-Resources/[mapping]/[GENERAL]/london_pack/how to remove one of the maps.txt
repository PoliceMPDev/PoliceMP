how to delete one of the maps 

to  delete the shard 

in this location london_pack\stream\optional_maps_delete  delete SHARD  folder here 

-----------------------


to  delete the legion  

in this location london_pack\stream\optional_maps_delete  delete LEGION   folder here 


------------
and do one of this 2 ways  down 



if you delete only legion do this as well 

in this location london_pack\replace  copy  [delete_legion_need] folder and paste in to  london_pack\stream\map_data

OR  

if you delete shard and legion at same time  do this  

in this location london_pack\replace  copy  [delete_legion_shard_need] folder and paste in to  london_pack\stream\map_data
